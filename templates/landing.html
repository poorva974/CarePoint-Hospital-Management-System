Core Modules

Designed for Healthcare Excellence

CarePoint unifies administrative workflows, clinical documentation, and revenue management into one secure system.

Patient Management

Comprehensive patient registration, demographic tracking, phone/name search, and individual patient timelines showing complete appointment and billing histories.

Doctor Management

Maintain specialist directories across departments (Cardiology, Neurology, Pediatrics, etc.), track contact details, and evaluate physician workload with consultation analytics.

Appointment Scheduling

Conflict-aware appointment booking preventing doctor double-booking and past dates. Support for real-time status updates (Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled).

Medical Records

Detailed electronic health records storing diagnoses, prescriptions, and lab examination reports with strict relational foreign-key validation linking patient, doctor, and appointment.

Billing & Invoices

Itemized billing invoices with payment status tracking (Paid, Pending, Partially Paid), insurance notations, aggregate revenue summaries, and printable official receipts.

Relational DBMS Integrity

Guaranteed referential integrity preventing accidental orphan records, parameterized queries eliminating SQL injection, password hashing, and session authentication.

About CarePoint

Engineered for Reliability and Compliance

CarePoint is developed as a comprehensive DBMS mini-project showcasing industry-standard relational database design and server-side engineering. The platform eliminates manual paper records, enforces data consistency, and optimizes hospital resource utilization.

Normalized Relational Schema

Structured in 3rd Normal Form (3NF) to minimize redundancy across patients, staff, consultations, and finance.

ACID Transactions & Foreign Key Constraints

Safe cascade validation protects historical medical and billing records from inadvertent data loss.

Role-Based Authentication & CSRF Shield

Cryptographic scrypt password hashing and session-bound CSRF token verification on all mutation operations.

MySQL 8.0 Python 3.14 / Flask Jinja2 Engine SCrypt Hashing Parameterized Queries
